Task 03 15 Marks 1-Dimensional Layouts

CSS Flexbox Layout

Flexbox (Flexible Box Layout Module) delivers powerful one-dimensional layout control along a main axis and cross axis. Seamlessly align, distribute, reorder, and scale items regardless of screen size.

Flex Container (display: flex) Flex Direction (row/column/reverse) Justify Content (6 Modes) Align Items (Cross Axis) Gap Spacing (gap: 1.5rem) Flex Items (grow/shrink/basis) Horizontal & Vertical Centering Responsive Flex Cards Section

04. Flex Item Properties: Grow, Shrink & Basis

Child items can dictate how available free space is partitioned with flex-grow, flex-shrink, flex-basis, and override alignment with align-self.

flex-basis: 180px
grow: 0 (Fixed width)
flex: 1 1 200px
flex-grow: 1 (Consumes 1 unit)
flex: 2 1 200px
flex-grow: 2 (Consumes 2 units)
align-self: flex-end

05. Real-World Component: Responsive Flex Cards

Arrangement of modern feature cards built with display: flex, flex-wrap: wrap, and gap: 1.5rem. Resize your screen to watch cards smoothly wrap and equalize height!
